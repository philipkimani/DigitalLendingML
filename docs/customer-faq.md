# Your loan and limit: questions and answers

This describes a demonstration lending service. All customers and figures are made up.

## Payments

**When is my payment due?** Your due date is set when your loan is paid out. The assistant can look up your next due date and amount.

**What if I'm late?** Pay as soon as you can. Late payments lower your on-time record, which affects whether you can get a higher limit.

**Can I pay early?** Yes. Paying early counts as paying on time.

## Increasing your limit

**When can my limit be reviewed?** About 60 days after your last loan was paid out. The assistant can tell you your date.

**What can I do to get a higher limit?** The main thing you control is repaying on time, every time. Your on-time record is the share of your past payments made by their due date. Higher records improve your chances, with the biggest steps at 85% and 95%.

**Does a good record guarantee an increase?** No. Each review also depends on the expected value of the increase and on how much the lender can offer that cycle. Sometimes an increase isn't available even with a good record.

**How many increases can I get?** There is a yearly limit on increases. Each one adds a share of your first loan amount.

**Can I turn down an increase?** Yes. You can decline an offer, and your existing loan isn't affected.

## This assistant

**Can you change my loan or accept an offer for me?** No. The assistant can answer questions, but it can't make payments, accept offers or change anything on your account.
